About Me

Hello! I'm Jacky Mpoka, an IT student and systems engineer based in Pretoria. My journey is rooted in a deep passion for low-level software engineering, operating system architecture, and building performant tools from the ground up. I am currently pursuing a BSc in Information Technology at the University of Pretoria, majoring in Data Science & Computer Science.

My work ranges from engineering AxisOS (a Debian-based Linux distribution with a native C++/Qt 6 suite) and contributing upstream C fixes to Linus Torvalds' MicroEMACS, to developing cycle-accurate CPU emulators, bare-metal ARM firmware, and interactive simulators. I combine rigorous hands-on problem solving with experience from WeThinkCode and the FNB App Academy.

Core Technologies:

Linux & Wayland C / C++20 Python Qt 6 Compilers & Emulators ARM & Embedded Drivers Computer Vision Git & Open Source CMake & Build Systems Godot & GDScript
